# Gila Gallery

The Gila Gallery, established in 2019, offers a fine selection of framed photographs and matted prints. The gallery features 15+ New Mexico's finest photographers, including Wayne Suggs, David Turning, Robert Brown, Pam Corner, Jorge Lizarraga, Jeannie Templeton, Al Lozano, Sylvia Armijo, and Timothy Baca. The gallery also selects handmade resin jewelry and ceramics, plus postcards, greeting cards, t-shirts, and Blue Q socks, oven mitts, and dish towels. We are at 206 N. Bullard in Silver City (right next to Little Toad Creek Brewery & Distillery).
